## 1. Executive summary

What was reviewed, what was not reviewed, key risks in plain language, and the three most important improvements. Avoid blanket statements such as "secure" or "hack-proof."

## 2. Scope and methodology

Exact authorized systems and accounts, assessment methods, approved testing window, no-go areas, tooling versions, constraints, limitations, and material deviations from the authorization.
